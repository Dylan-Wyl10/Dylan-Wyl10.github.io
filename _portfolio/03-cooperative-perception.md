## Phase 1: A Cooperative Perception System for CAV Navigation and Improving Safety
*Funded by the NSF award "CPS: Medium: Collaborative Research: Transforming Connected and Automated Transportation with Smart Networking, Cooperative Sensing, and Edge Computing"*

- **Objective**: implement a cooperative perception system to improve road safety.
- **Contributions**:
  1. Built a cooperative perception system that connects roadside LiDAR and the sensors of connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) through V2X.
  2. Developed a detection and navigation algorithm on the CAV with a sensor data fusion pipeline.
  3. Built and ran field tests demonstrating the safety benefits of detecting occluded vulnerable road users (VRUs) through the cooperative perception system.
- **Findings**: the cooperative perception system helps CAVs plan safer trajectories (higher post-encroachment time) and smoother ones (lower deceleration rates).
- **Publication**: Hanlin Chen, Vamsi K Bandaru, Yilin Wang, Mario A Romero, Andrew Tarko, Yiheng Feng (2024). "[Cooperative Perception System for Aiding Connected and Automated Vehicle Navigation and Improving Safety](https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981241252779)." *Transportation Research Record*, 2678(12), 1498-1510.

## Phase 2: An Infrastructure-Vehicle Cooperative Perception System
*Jul 2024 – Jun 2025. Funded by the CCAT grant "Cost Benefit Analysis of an Infrastructure-Vehicle Cooperative Perception System"*

- **Objective**: develop a cooperative perception system that includes multiple vehicles, infrastructure sensors, and drones.
- **Methodology**: multi-sensor fusion of infrastructure sensors, drone-view cameras, and vehicle sensors.
- **Contribution**: extracted and processed multi-sensor data (LiDAR, camera, and others) into vehicle trajectories; an open-source dataset is being prepared for public release.
