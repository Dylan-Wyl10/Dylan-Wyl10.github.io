## Overview
An internal exploration of a "safety instinct" for Physical AI: a safety cerebellum for a Boston Dynamics Spot robot, built in NVIDIA Isaac Sim (Omniverse).

## Methodology
A reinforcement learning (RL) waypoint-following controller was trained for Spot, and a Control Barrier Function (CBF) safety filter was layered beneath it: the learned policy handles navigation while the CBF layer enforces safety.

## Contribution
Spot maintains a dynamic safety boundary around moving obstacles while traveling between waypoints.

## Tools
NVIDIA Isaac Sim (Omniverse), reinforcement learning, Control Barrier Functions
