At the [IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV 2026)](https://ieee-iv.org/2026/) (June 22-25, 2026, Detroit, MI), I gave an MMITSS transit signal priority demonstration at Mcity in Ann Arbor for symposium attendees and USDOT officials.

MMITSS (Multi-Modal Intelligent Traffic Signal System) is the open-source connected-vehicle signal priority system that I redeveloped for the Michigan deployments; details are on the [project page]({{ '/portfolio/05-v2x-tsc/' | relative_url }}).
