Our CART Lab at Purdue University was invited to the University of Michigan Transportation Research Institute (UMTRI) 60th Anniversary Symposium (Oct 2-3, 2025, Ann Arbor, MI), where I gave a live demonstration of a Transit Signal Priority (TSP) system enabled by Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication.

## The Demo

A transit bus equipped with V2X on-board units drove through Mcity, showcasing how TSP leverages V2X communication to enable smarter, more adaptive traffic signals and to improve mobility and efficiency for priority vehicles in real-world traffic.

## Real-World Deployment

The same system has served Route 6 transit buses along the Ellsworth corridor in southern Ann Arbor since August 2025. It was developed in the USDOT FHWA project **Smart Intersections: Paving the Way for a National CAV Deployment**; more on the system on the [MMITSS project page]({{ '/portfolio/05-v2x-tsc/' | relative_url }}).
