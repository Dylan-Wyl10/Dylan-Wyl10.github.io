The TeraSim demo with NVIDIA and the City of San Jose was selected for NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's keynote at [GTC 2026](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/gtc-2026-news/), delivered on March 16, 2026, in San Jose, CA.

I led this city-scale urban simulation demo during my internship as Simulation Technical Leader Intern at Inchor (SaferDrive AI). Built under the NVIDIA Metropolis Blueprint, it combines an integrated "City Operations Agent" and a digital twin that together perceive, simulate, and optimize traffic operations. I designed its full simulation pipeline, including city-scale vehicle injection, dynamic routing, and multi-phase demo orchestration.

NVIDIA's [GTC 2026 press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-t-mobile-and-partners-integrate-physical-ai-applications-on-ai-ran-ready-infrastructure) of the same day names Inchor among the developers testing "City Operations Agents" and a digital twin for the City of San Jose.
