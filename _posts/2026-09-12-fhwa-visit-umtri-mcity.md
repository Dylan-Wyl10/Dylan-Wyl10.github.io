[UMTRI's Jul 30, 2026 report](https://www.umtri.umich.edu/2026/07/30/umtri-and-mcity-welcome-federal-highway-administration-delegates/) covers a visit by Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) delegates to the Ann Arbor Connected Environment (AACE) and the Mcity Test Facility. The visit highlighted connected and automated vehicle testing and a live demonstration of a near-miss alert system that detects potential conflicts and warns road users in real time.

The article also includes a photo of me beside a research bus at Mcity.
